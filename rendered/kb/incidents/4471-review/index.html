Two different failures

Parser reads month first for day-first tenants

The shared parser reads month first. For tenants whose locale is day first, app and email reports, and reports through the older booking form, classify as parser. For example, R-41200 describes 7 April becoming 4 July at Harbour Dental. The export establishes the classification; it does not show a deployed fix.

Booking form shows a hint that disagrees with its reader

The newer booking form prints MM/DD/YYYY, but reads the input in the tenant’s format. For a day-first tenant this classifies as hint, a separate failure. R-41230 is a form report from Copper Studio: 6 January becomes 1 June. Correct the hint and input interpretation together; a shared-parser fix alone does not establish that this path is fixed.
